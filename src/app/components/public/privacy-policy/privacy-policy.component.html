Privacy Policy

At Rudbeckia by Rebecca, I am committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of my clients, event coordinators, and anyone involved in the planning process. This privacy policy outlines how I collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you interact with my website and services.

  1. Information I Collect: I collect information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, event details, coordinator information, venue addresses, and preferences provided through contact forms, inquiry submissions, or consultations.
  2. How I Use Information: I use the collected data to plan and fulfill floral services, create proposals and invoices, coordinate with vendors, and enhance your overall experience.
  3. Data Sharing: I do not sell or share personal information with third parties. Information may be shared only with trusted vendors or team members directly involved in fulfilling your event, and only as necessary.
  4. Photographs and Publicity: With your permission, I may use event photos that include my floral designs on my website, social media, and in marketing materials. You may withdraw this permission at any time by contacting me.
  5. Storage and Security: I store all client data securely using encrypted systems and password protection. I take reasonable measures to protect this information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
  6. Cookies and Tracking: My website may use cookies and analytics to improve functionality and user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.
  7. Data Retention: I retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including any legal, tax, or archival obligations.
  8. Your Rights: You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information. To do so, please contact me directly and I will assist with your request.

Thank you for trusting me with your event and data. If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, I invite you to reach out so I can address them personally.
